The Blue Whale of Colombo

This skeleton has been displayed at the Colombo National Museum since 1914. It is one of only a handful of complete blue whale skeletons on public display in Asia.

Anatomy of a Giant

01
Rostrum (Upper Jaw)
The rostrum of a blue whale contains baleen plates — comb-like structures used to filter krill from seawater. A single blue whale can consume 40 million krill per day.
02
Pectoral Fins
The pectoral fins are used for steering and stabilisation. Inside each fin are five finger-like bones — evidence of the whale's land-dwelling ancestors from 50 million years ago.
03
Tail Flukes
Unlike fish, whale flukes move vertically. A single thrust of the flukes can accelerate the whale to 50 km/h in bursts — enough power to outswim most ocean predators.
