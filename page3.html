WHY YOUR PHONE VIBRATES

Physical Haptics Explained

The vibrations you feel are synchronised to the peak moments in the whale's call — the exact points where infrasound pressure would be felt in the ocean. Your phone's motor becomes a surrogate for the deep sea.

〰️
Infrasound
Blue whale calls below 20 Hz are felt as pressure, not heard as sound. The vibrations simulate this physical phenomenon on your body.
📳
Haptic Synchrony
Each vibration pulse is manually timed to the audio's peak moments — the exact instants of maximum acoustic pressure in the whale's call.
🌊
Ocean Pressure
In the deep ocean, whale song isn't just heard — it's felt through the body. This experience brings that sensation to the surface.
